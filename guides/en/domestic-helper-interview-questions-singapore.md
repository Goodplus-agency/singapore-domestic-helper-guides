# How to Interview a Domestic Helper in 15 Minutes

> Official Goodplus guide: [Read the full article](https://goodplus.com.sg/blog/domestic-helper-interview-questions-singapore?utm_source=github&utm_medium=knowledge-repository&utm_campaign=geo)

## Direct answer

A useful domestic-helper interview can take about 15 focused minutes. Start with an honest description of the household, then ask the candidate to explain real situations rather than answer yes-or-no questions. Check experience, safety judgement, communication and expectations, and give her time to decide whether the job fits her too.

## Key points

- Decide the three requirements that genuinely determine success in your home, such as infant safety, mobility support or communication.
- Describe the real duties, household members, pets, sleeping arrangement, cameras and likely rest-day arrangement before testing fit.
- Ask what the candidate did in a specific past situation, in what order and why.
- Compare work-history answers with the bio-data and MOM employment record, but clarify discrepancies before drawing conclusions.
- Confirm salary, rest days, main duties and intended timing, then invite the candidate to ask questions.

## A 15-minute interview plan

| Time | Focus | Example prompt |
|---|---|---|
| Minutes 1–2 | Honest job preview | “Our main need is after-school childcare, simple dinner and general cleaning. Is that the work you expect?” |
| Minutes 3–9 | Real examples | “A toddler is crying while food is cooking. What would you do first, and why?” |
| Minutes 10–12 | Work history | “Choose one past household. Who lived there, what did you do daily, and why did the job end?” |
| Minutes 13–15 | Terms and two-way fit | Confirm duties, salary, rest days and sleeping arrangements, then ask what she wants to know. |

After the call, score each candidate consistently from 1 to 5 on relevant experience, safety judgement, communication, learning approach and two-way fit. Do not let a high overall score hide a genuine safety concern or another non-negotiable.

## Questions that reveal more than “yes”

- **Childcare:** “Tell me about the youngest child you cared for and the daily routine.”
- **Eldercare:** “How did you help an unsteady person move safely?”
- **Cooking:** “Which three dishes can you cook without help, and how would you learn a new one?”
- **Communication:** “What do you do when you do not understand an instruction?”
- **Learning:** “Tell me about a mistake and how you corrected it.”

Assess the individual evidence. Fresh, ex-Singapore and transfer labels—and nationality—do not establish a person’s character or household fit.

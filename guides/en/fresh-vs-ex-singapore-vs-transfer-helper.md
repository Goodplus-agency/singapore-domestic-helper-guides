# Fresh vs Ex-Singapore vs Transfer Helper

> Official Goodplus guide: [Read the full article](https://goodplus.com.sg/blog/fresh-vs-ex-singapore-vs-transfer-helper?utm_source=github&utm_medium=knowledge-repository&utm_campaign=geo)

## Direct answer

Choose by evidence, timing and household fit—not by a label. A Fresh helper usually needs more overseas and arrival steps; an ex-Singapore helper has a local employment record but is overseas; and a transfer helper is already in Singapore but can start sooner only when employer consent, Work Permit validity and transfer dates align.

## Quick comparison

| Starting point | What it usually means | Evidence and interview | Timing tendency |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fresh | Has not worked as an MDW in Singapore | Usually a video interview; ask separately about family care, training and paid work | Usually longest because overseas and first-arrival steps remain |
| Ex-Singapore | Worked in Singapore before and is now overseas | Video interview plus a Singapore employment record that can be checked | Often between Fresh and transfer, but overseas processing still applies |
| Transfer | Is in Singapore and moving to a new employer | Video or possible in-person interview; discuss the record and transfer reason | Often shortest only when consent, permit and handover dates align |

These are common market descriptions, not official grades of ability or character. MOM defines a first-time helper as someone who has never worked in Singapore as a helper, including someone with an employment record who ultimately did not work here.

## What to verify

### Fresh helper

- Ask for specific examples from household care, training or paid employment.
- If MOM treats her as a first-time helper, the Settling-In Programme is required.
- Allow for source-country documents, travel clearance, flights and arrival steps.
- Some Fresh candidates may not yet have a passport; passport application and approval add extra waiting time.
- Plan additional time to demonstrate and reinforce your household routines.

### Ex-Singapore helper

- Check the MOM employment record through FDW eService or ask the employment agency to check.
- Ask about actual duties, contract duration and reasons for leaving; the record alone does not explain these.
- Remember that overseas travel, insurance, arrival and Work Permit steps still apply.
- Ex-Singapore candidates usually already hold a valid passport from previous overseas work, which can shorten the preparation timeline.

### Transfer helper

- Confirm that any due six-monthly medical examination has been completed.
- For a direct transfer, MOM says the initial steps must be completed while the current Work Permit has more than 30 days’ validity.
- The current employer must approve the transfer request.
- MOM states that the application outcome is generally available within one week. If approved, the new Work Permit must be issued by the earlier of 21 days from approval or the current permit’s expiry.
- Transfers come in two scenarios: the helper is still under the current employer’s name and transfers directly, or she has already been transferred to the agency’s name. MOM must approve the new Work Permit in either case; if she is already under the agency’s name, she must also visit the MOM Services Centre for registration and fingerprinting.
- Do not promise a start date until approval and the agreed handover date are confirmed.

## A practical decision rule

- **Earliest realistic start:** shortlist transfer helpers, then verify every permit, consent and handover date.
- **Verifiable Singapore experience with time for travel:** compare ex-Singapore candidates.
- **Time and capacity to teach from the beginning:** consider Fresh candidates.
- **Specialised care:** prioritise demonstrated infant, mobility or dementia-care experience over every category label.

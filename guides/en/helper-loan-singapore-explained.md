# What Is a Helper Loan in Singapore?

> Official Goodplus guide: [Read the full article](https://goodplus.com.sg/blog/helper-loan-singapore-explained?utm_source=github&utm_medium=knowledge-repository&utm_campaign=geo)

## Direct answer

A “helper loan” is commonly an amount an employer advances on a domestic helper’s behalf for preparation costs such as training and source-country processing. It is separate from the agency service fee and third-party hiring costs. Before paying, obtain the exact amount, a written month-by-month repayment schedule and the early-termination terms for the selected helper.

## Separate the three figures

| Quotation item | What it generally represents | What to ask for |
|---|---|---|
| Agency service fee | Matching, interviews, paperwork and support | Scope, replacement terms and refund conditions |
| Processing and third-party costs | Insurance, MOM fees, medical checks, transport, lodging, flights or programmes where applicable | Itemised amount and whether each item is refundable |
| Helper loan | Advance made on the helper’s behalf under the agreed arrangement | Exact balance, monthly schedule and early-termination treatment |

The helper-loan amount is not the same for every person. It can vary with source-country arrangements, whether the helper is a first-time, ex-Singapore or local transfer candidate, and how much has already been repaid. Compare complete candidate-specific totals, not only an advertised agency fee.

## Check the written schedule

The employer and helper should be able to see the same figures for every month:

- the basic salary declared and agreed;
- the scheduled repayment amount;
- the amount paid to the helper;
- any separate rest-day compensation; and
- the balance remaining after that month.

Keep the signed schedule with the employment contract and salary records. MOM requires an MDW’s salary to be paid monthly, no later than seven days after the salary period, and not below the amount declared to MOM. If salary is paid in cash, both parties must sign each payment record. An employer must not keep the MDW’s money or bank card.

Rest-day compensation is separate. At least one rest day each month cannot be compensated away. If both parties agree that the helper will work on other rest days, MOM requires at least one day’s salary on top of the monthly salary for each such day.

## Five questions before paying

1. What is the complete candidate-specific total, including the helper loan?
2. Where is the exact loan amount recorded?
3. Can both parties review the full monthly repayment and salary schedule?
4. How will any remaining balance be handled if employment ends early or the helper transfers?
5. Which refund, offset and replacement terms are written in the signed service agreement?

Do not rely on verbal estimates or change deductions independently.
